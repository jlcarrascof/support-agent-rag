# Payment Methods

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and in-app wallet balance. Cash payment is available only for food delivery orders in select cities, not for rides.

If a payment fails after an order is placed, the order is automatically cancelled and no charge is made. Customers sometimes see a temporary "pending" charge from their bank — this is an authorization hold, not an actual charge, and it disappears within 24-48 hours if the order was cancelled.

Adding a new payment method requires verifying it with a small temporary charge (less than $1), which is refunded immediately after verification.
