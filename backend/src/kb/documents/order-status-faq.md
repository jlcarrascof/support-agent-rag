# Order Status FAQ

Orders go through four stages: placed, preparing, in transit, and delivered. You can check your order status at any time by giving the support agent your order number.

If your order has been "preparing" for more than 20 minutes without moving to "in transit," this usually means the restaurant is experiencing high demand. The estimated delivery time will automatically adjust in these cases.

If your order shows "delivered" but you haven't received it, wait 5 minutes (the courier may be at the wrong door) before reporting it as missing.
