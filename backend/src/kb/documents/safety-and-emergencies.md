# Safety and Emergencies

In case of an emergency during a ride or delivery, customers should contact local emergency services first, then report the incident through the app so the safety team can follow up.

The app includes an in-ride safety button that shares live location with a trusted contact and, in select regions, directly with local authorities.

Support agents are trained to treat any message mentioning safety concerns, harassment, or accidents with priority, escalating immediately to the safety team instead of handling it as a standard support case.
