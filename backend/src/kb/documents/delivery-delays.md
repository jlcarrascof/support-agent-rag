# Delivery Delays

Delays can happen due to high demand, weather conditions, or restaurant preparation time. The app updates the estimated delivery time in real time, so the current estimate is always more accurate than the one shown when the order was placed.

If an order is more than 45 minutes late compared to the original estimate, the customer is automatically eligible for a partial refund of the delivery fee, even without requesting it.

Severe delays (more than 90 minutes) qualify for a full refund if the customer requests it within 24 hours of the delay.
