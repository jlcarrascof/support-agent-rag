# Ride Cancellation Policy

Rides can be cancelled free of charge up to 5 minutes after booking, or any time before a driver has been assigned.

If a driver has already been assigned and is en route, cancelling the ride incurs a small cancellation fee to compensate the driver for the trip to the pickup point. This fee is waived if the driver is more than 10 minutes late to the agreed pickup time.

A ride already marked "in progress" (the passenger is in the vehicle) cannot be cancelled through support — it must be completed or ended by the driver.
