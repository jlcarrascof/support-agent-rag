# Restaurant Partner Issues

If an item is missing from an order, support can issue a partial refund for that specific item without requiring a refund of the entire order.

Repeated quality complaints about a specific restaurant are tracked separately from individual order refunds — they feed into a partner quality score that can lead to the restaurant being temporarily removed from the platform.

Closed or unavailable restaurants shown as "open" in the app are usually a sync delay; support can manually flag the restaurant as closed to prevent further orders while the issue is investigated.
