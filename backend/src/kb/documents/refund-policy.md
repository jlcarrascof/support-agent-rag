# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of order delivery if the order arrived damaged, incomplete, or significantly delayed (more than 45 minutes past the estimated delivery time).

Refunds are processed to the original payment method and typically appear within 3-5 business days. An order can only be refunded once — a second refund request on the same order will be rejected automatically.

Refunds are not available for orders marked as delivered and consumed, unless there is clear evidence of a quality issue reported within 24 hours of delivery.
