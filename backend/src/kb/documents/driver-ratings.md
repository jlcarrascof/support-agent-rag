# Driver Ratings and Feedback

Customers rate drivers from 1 to 5 stars after each ride. Ratings below 3 stars prompt an optional comment, which is reviewed by the support team but does not immediately affect the driver's account.

Drivers whose average rating drops below a set threshold over their last 50 rides are flagged for a quality review, which may include additional training or, in repeated cases, suspension.

A single low rating never results in automatic driver suspension — only sustained patterns do.
